Case Studies

Selected work from my career in pricing, reserving, capital and financial reporting. Each case is described by type of organisation; the roles themselves are listed under Experience.

  1. Insurance group with MGA and carrier businesses2025 – present

    A reserving framework built from the ground up

    Context

    The group, writing around USD 1bn of gross premium, needed a structured, documented quarterly reserving process for its MGA lines of business.

    Work

    Designed the methodology, controls and documentation and set up the quarterly cycle. I run the quarterly reserving across the MGA lines and supervise the subsidiaries' quarterly reserving submissions.

    Result

    A documented quarterly reserving cycle across the MGA lines, with a consistent review of subsidiary submissions.

    • Chain ladder, BF, ELR
    • Reserving governance
    • Quarterly close

  4. Lloyd's and London-listed specialty insurer2022 – 2024

    IFRS 17 implementation

    Context

    The group was implementing IFRS 17 while its enterprise calculation engine was still being finalised.

    Work

    Built the interim Excel IFRS 17 balance-sheet model. Developed gross and reinsurance Solvency II to IFRS 17 reconciliations with variance analysis, and validated ULR and ENID assumptions on GAAP, Solvency II and IFRS 17 bases.

    Result

    The interim model was used for Group and Audit Committee reporting, and IFRS 17 went live with the team I was part of.

    • IFRS 17
    • Solvency II reconciliation
    • ULR and ENID
