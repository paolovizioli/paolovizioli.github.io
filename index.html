Profile

Pricing, reserving and capital, with a finance background

I am a senior London Market actuary covering pricing, reserving and capital across Lloyd's syndicates and managing agents, MGAs, P&I and specialty carriers. I currently act as de facto Deputy Chief Actuary at MNK Group, where I co-lead the group pricing transformation and built and run the MGA reserving framework.

Before moving into actuarial work I spent a decade in valuation, strategy and FP&A at PwC, Aviva and Prudential. I am at home building the model and explaining the answer to boards, audit and reserve committees, auditors, capital providers and rating agencies, and suited to roles where one person covers the whole actuarial function.

Lloyd's planning

Syndicate business forecasts, QMB and QMA, QSR, ASR and TPD returns, loss ratio reviews, re-forecasting and start-up syndicate loss ratios.

Corporate finance

Due diligence, valuation, business planning and five-year group plans; modelling for capital providers. Tools: Excel and VBA, SQL, R, Python.
